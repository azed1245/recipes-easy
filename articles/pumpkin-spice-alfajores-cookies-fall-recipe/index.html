Pumpkin Spice Alfajores Cookies Fall Recipe

October 17, 2026

Fall is the perfect season for cozy treats, and these Pumpkin Spice Alfajores take the classic South American shortbread cookie to a whole new level. Imagine buttery, melt-in-your-mouth cookies infused with warm autumn spices and a hint of real pumpkin, sandwiched together with rich, creamy dulce de leche. They are the ultimate indulgence to enjoy as the leaves begin to change.

Total Time: 1 hour 42 minutes (includes chilling time)

Servings: 24 cookies

Difficulty Level: Medium
